# Lucide control icons

These SVGs are vendored from [Lucide](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide) at revision `a630cec77eac6568b989e87e4947a8bfe45caacd`. They are also available in the [Lucide collection on Iconify](https://icon-sets.iconify.design/lucide/).

Source files: `https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/tree/a630cec77eac6568b989e87e4947a8bfe45caacd/icons`

The original SVGs are retained unchanged. The renderer resolves their monochrome `currentColor` to white when rasterizing and applies the UI tint when painting. Source SVGs, copyright notices, and ISC/MIT terms are bundled in this directory. The full [LICENSE](LICENSE) is also embedded in the application and accessible in Preferences → About → Icon licenses.

The `Icon` mapping lives in `src/ui/icons.rs`. `Scan` is used for fitting the image, `Ratio` for actual pixels, and `Gallery thumbnails` for the thumbnail-strip toggle. Flowa's flower logo remains its own artwork.
